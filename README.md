The selection started from gameplay features shared with Assassin’s Creed: infiltration, assassinations, traversal, exploration, melee combat and progression. It now also lists **every released game in each series that appears here**, so some spin-offs (card games, puzzle games, tactical shooters) share only the name — the *Style* and *Shared features* columns say so plainly.

**What counts as a separate game:** remakes rebuilt from scratch get their own row (Black Flag – Resynced, Metal Gear Solid Delta, Demon’s Souls 2020…). Remasters, ports, compilations and expansions do not. Mobile-only titles are left out, except for Metal Gear, which is listed in full. Unreleased or cancelled games (Lords of the Fallen II, the Sands of Time remake) are not included.

**Player scores:** SensCritique community averages out of 10, checked September 17, 2026. They are player ratings, not critic scores, and they change over time. Click a score to open the game’s page; *Look up* opens a SensCritique search for games whose page wasn’t confirmed.
