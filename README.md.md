# Assassin’s Creed, Souls, The Witcher and related games

**70 games — 42 additions to the original 28-game table.** Includes 16 Assassin’s Creed entries and 54 related games, from older classics to newer releases.

The selection is based on combinations of gameplay features. The stealth groups emphasize infiltration, assassinations, traversal and combat. The action-RPG and Souls groups emphasize exploration, melee combat and character progression; many do not have Assassin’s Creed-style parkour or social stealth. The “Shared features” column makes the combination explicit for every game.

**Player scores:** SensCritique community averages out of 10, checked September 16, 2026. These are player ratings, not critic scores; they can change. Click each score for the exact game/edition page.

**Images:** Each row contains a real game screenshot and cover art. Screenshots are sourced from walkthroughs, gameplay/display reports and player screenshot galleries. Tenchu uses an animated gameplay GIF. Click either image for its source. Images are externally hosted and require an internet connection. Original releases and remasters are not counted as separate games here.
